🔌 IntelliSpace Hardware Guide

Visual step-by-step instructions to connect your ESP8266, Relay, Fan & LED

💡

How It Works

The 2-channel relay is just an automatic light switch. When you toggle a button in your mobile app, the ESP8266 triggers the relay with a loud "CLICK" sound, which powers the fan or lights up the LED!

1
Connect Relay Module to NodeMCU (4 Wires)

Use 4 jumper wires to connect the signal pins on the NodeMCU to the input pins on the relay board:

NodeMCU Vin ➔ Relay VCC
5V Power Supply
NodeMCU GND ➔ Relay GND
Ground
NodeMCU D1 ➔ Relay IN1
Fan Control Signal
NodeMCU D2 ➔ Relay IN2
Light Control Signal
2
Connect 5V Fan to Relay Channel 1

Look at the green/blue screw terminal on Relay 1 (has 3 holes: NO, COM, NC):

NodeMCU Vin ➔ Relay 1 COM (Middle)
Tighten screw
Relay 1 NO ➔ Fan RED Wire (+)
Normally Open
Fan BLACK Wire (-) ➔ NodeMCU GND
Ground Loop
⚠️ Is your Fan running continuously without stopping?
Make sure the Fan RED wire is screwed into NO (Normally Open), NOT NC (Normally Closed). If it is in NC, power flows 24/7 even when unpowered. If your fan is spinning non-stop right now, simply move the red wire to the opposite outer hole!
3
Connect 5V LED Module to Relay Channel 2

Look at the screw terminal on Relay 2 (the second channel):

NodeMCU Vin ➔ Relay 2 COM (Middle)
Tighten screw
Relay 2 NO ➔ LED Positive / Signal (+)
Light Power
LED Ground (-) ➔ NodeMCU GND
Ground Loop
4
Upload Code with Arduino IDE

Open hardware/esp8266_firmware/esp8266_firmware.ino in Arduino IDE:

const char* WIFI_SSID = "YOUR_WIFI_OR_HOTSPOT_NAME";
const char* WIFI_PASSWORD = "YOUR_WIFI_PASSWORD";
  1. Plug NodeMCU into your PC with the USB cable.
  2. Select Board: Tools ➔ Board ➔ ESP8266 ➔ NodeMCU 1.0 (ESP-12E Module).
  3. Select Port: Tools ➔ Port ➔ COM3 / COM4.
  4. Click the Upload (➔) button and wait for "Done uploading".
5
Test with Your Mobile App!

You are all set! Now open your IntelliSpace mobile app:

  • Go to More ➔ Lab Control
  • Turn ON "Exhaust Ventilation Fan" ➔ The blue relay clicks and the Fan starts spinning!
  • Turn ON "Main Ceiling Lights" ➔ The second relay clicks and the LED lights up!
  • Watch the Energy Monitoring screen update in real-time as power draw increases!
